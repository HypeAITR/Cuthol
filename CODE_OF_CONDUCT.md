# Code of conduct

Cuthol is a small project with contributors from many places and backgrounds. We want it to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a bug or send a change without being treated badly.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Disagree about code and decisions, not about people. Give reasons and measurements.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through **Security → Report a vulnerability** (marked "conduct") or to the maintainer's GitHub account. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules, in proportion to what happened.

This document is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
